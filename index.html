Intelligent Weather Insights, Right When You Need Them

SkyCast connects conversational AI with live meteorological data. Ask about real-time temperatures, rain probability, and smart outfit advice for any city across the globe.

Click the weather launcher in the bottom right corner to begin chatting!
🌍

Global Geocoding & Forecasts

Queries Open-Meteo's geocoding REST API to resolve city coordinates on the fly, then fetches live temperature, conditions, and wind speed.

☔

Rain & Umbrella Alerts

Evaluates real-time precipitation probability and WMO weather codes to provide an instant yes/no verdict on taking an umbrella before heading out.

🧥

Smart Outfit Advice

Uses temperature thresholds to provide comfort-calibrated clothing recommendations—from warm layers under 15°C to light, breathable fabrics above 24°C.

Sample Prompts to Try

Test SkyCast's conversational actions and live API responses directly in the chat widget:

"What's the weather in Lagos?" "Do I need an umbrella in London today?" "What should I wear in Benin City?" "What is the forecast in Abuja?" "Check weather in Ekpoma"
